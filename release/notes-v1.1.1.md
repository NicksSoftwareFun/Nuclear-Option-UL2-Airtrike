UL-2 Airtrike 1.1.1: Balance Pass.

**Install:** put `UL2Airtrike.dll` in `BepInEx\plugins`, replacing the old one. Requires BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter. If you have a UL-2 TEST BUILD installed, remove it first (both use the same plugin ID).

**Balance**
- **40mm High Velocity grenades** for the seat 3 Grenadier and the Special Operations Team: muzzle velocity 400 m/s (less flight time against moving convoys) and a bigger blast that can hurt Linebreakers. Special Operations Team grenades no longer burst in the air before reaching targets near their 3,300 m maximum range.
- **Harder hitting .338** for the seat 3 Machine Gunner and the Special Operations Team machine gunners (pierce 150 -> 355): they can now knock out MRAPs and light trucks out to about 3 km.
- Special Operations Team grenadiers spread their lead less (5% instead of 10%) and correct their aim from where their rounds land.
- With the Special Operations Team aboard, the centre of gravity sits further forward, so the airtrike no longer tips back onto its tail as easily.

**New**
- Longer barrel with an open bore and a boxy optic (flip-down front cover, top camera, D-pad) on the grenade launchers.
- **Eyeball Mk.II x2** on the stub pylons. **Hangball Mk.II x2** also appears there when the Hangball Mk.II mod is installed (not required).
- The AI can fly the Special Operations Team.
