UL-2 Airtrike 1.1.0: the Special Operations Team update.

**Install:** put `UL2Airtrike.dll` in `BepInEx\plugins`, replacing the old one. Requires BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter. If you have a UL-2 TEST BUILD installed, remove it first (both use the same plugin ID).

**New**
- **Passenger Module: Special Operations Team.** One loadout pick that fills the third seat and both wing stubs with a 4-man team: two outward-facing .338 machine gunners back to back in the third seat row and a 40mm grenadier on each wing stub. The two machine guns and the two grenade launchers each fire as one weapon, with 50% more range than the single door gunner and 3 extra magazines each. Lands to capture like a full infantry bay. With only the team aboard the aircraft is at its 1200 kg max weight, so the nose gun is extra.
- **Delta Digital livery**, a four-tone pixel camo.
- AI pilots bank off the runway centreline after takeoff (alternating left and right) and climb out steeper, so the next aircraft taking off doesn't fly into them.

**Fixes**
- The seat 3 Machine Gunner and Grenadier can no longer shoot the crew. The gun stays on safety until the turret has swung onto its aim.
- The IRM-S2 no longer breaks the main gear off in the aircraft select screen or at spawn.
- The Expedient 1.5kt Bomb can is no longer see-through from the top and bottom.
- Gunner heads face where they aim, and leftover rifle pieces are gone from the seated soldiers.
- The tyres no longer sink into the ground.
