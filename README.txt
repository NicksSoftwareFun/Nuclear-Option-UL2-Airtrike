UL-2 Airtrike
=============

A three-seat armed gyrocopter for Nuclear Option, built with Blueprinter.
Based on the Shaanxi Baoji Lie Ying military gyrocopter.

Status: early work in progress. Nothing here is playable yet.

Planned
-------
- Pilot, gunner and a third seat that can carry a spotter, a machine gunner
  or a grenadier.
- Fixed 12.7 mm gun on the right side with a small self-aiming arc.
- Two side pylons for light stores.
- Crew bail out over the side. No ejection seats.

Layout
------
art/scripts   Blender scripts that build and render the model.
