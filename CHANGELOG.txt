UL-2 Airtrike changelog
=======================

Unreleased
- 0.15.9 test build: rotor hub spins with the blades; blade lift area x1.6 and drag roughly halved; rotor assist on
  the takeoff roll held to 25 m/s; jump gauge moved to the right middle of the HUD as a vertical bar in the stock HUD
  font and colour; flight assist on + full throttle on the ground starts an automatic jump; spawns at revetments and
  shelters as well as medium hangars; takeoff-roll log (speed, climb, yaw rate).
- 0.15.8 test build: spawns at medium hangars only (helipads off for runway testing); takeoff stability: rotor lift
  centred just behind the centre of mass, larger fins, gentler pedal brake on the main wheels, main tyres slide before
  they trip the narrow track, gun mount mass moved to the centreline. SUB-2000 raised out of the seat sill with cleaner
  stock and frame; sensor housing blended into the belly with darker lens rings.
- 0.15.7 test build: folded SUB-2000 carbines on seats 1 and 2; HUD pre-rotator gauge (rpm, idle-to-full bar, red to
  green at jump speed); rotor head pitch authority on the takeoff roll; mast, engine and tail braced to the frame, stiffer
  main gear, less nose steering lock; faired sensor turret housing with side arm; square MFD with smaller six-pack; gauges
  moved to the cockpit camera layer so they draw.
- 0.15.6 test build: prop no longer stops below full throttle (strike-detector disc kept clear of the engine part,
  strike log added); custom axis 1 scaled to the binding's range; main gear casts clear of the nacelle so the wheels roll;
  gunner idle pose upright; darker rifle furniture; harnesses hide with their occupant / gunner kit; side seam removed;
  AGM-48 / IRM-S2 rail mounts equip again on lower pylons; six-pack flight instruments around a larger MFD; targeting
  ball rebuilt with flush lens windows.
- 0.15.5 test build: fixed the engine RPM loop (prop blades struck the tail boom collider) and the tail snapping at spawn
  (overlapping colliders now jointed); colliders skip thin hardware. Cockpit rework: square-shouldered cowl, wrap-round
  windscreen, portrait MFD, smaller gauges. Mid-belly sensor turret, beefed-up stub pylons with direct AGM-48/IRM-S2
  rails, FDE seat rifles, parked ejection seat hidden on bail-out, part-detach log.
- Blockout model scripts.
