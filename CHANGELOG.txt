UL-2 Airtrike changelog
=======================

Unreleased
- 0.15.28: rotor blur now uses the stock gunship blurred blade pieces (five per blade, on both blades) and the prop uses the stock Cricket blur disc scaled to our prop, since copied blur materials never drew in game; liveries now reach the rudders, elevators and the side gun housing; Polished Stainless no longer shows a wavy band on the tail (smooth top-to-bottom shading).
- 0.15.27: rotor blur is now two blurred blade sweeps that turn with the rotor (the 0.15.26 disc was too faint to see); own prop blur disc replaces the Cricket's six-blade disc; spinner with a white tip and two white spiral stripes, turning at a capped rate so the spin always reads; blur materials copied from the stock ducted-fan blur.
- 0.15.26: rotor blur disc fades in with rotor rpm (the blades hide behind it, so paused screenshots keep the blur); prop blades wired into the stock prop so its blur disc covers them and a prop strike leaves broken stubs; rotor blades break where they hit the ground or anything solid (both blades lose that segment and everything outboard, the rotor stops, stock strike dust); engine rpm gauge replaces the turn coordinator; airspeed in km/h with yellow 200-240 and a red line at 240; compass card turns under a fixed red pointer; artificial horizon now banks the right way.
- 0.15.25: two new liveries, Polished Stainless (bright steel with mirror cues painted in, livery gloss 0.6) and Black Tiger (black with orange tiger stripes); seat 3 MG 338 fires a tracer every 3rd round (was 5).
- 0.15.24: airframe trimmed from 75.0k to 64.9k triangles with no visible change (flat areas merged, fewer pod and camera ball rings), seat 3 kits about 1% lighter; gun ammo box moved inside the pod (no longer pokes through the right stub); inner engine faces use the stock engine internals material; main rotor plays the stock rotor loop with pitch and volume following rotor rpm.
- 0.15.23: own map/selection icon and seat 3 weapon icons (MG, grenadier, spotter); seat 3 passenger adds capture power 2 (1/8 of a full Ibis troop load) while landed and stopped.
- 0.15.22: part joints break like the stock Cricket (scaled to weight); fire and break-up effects on the main structure, impact damage on the pod, mast, engine, booms and gear; skin normal and metallic maps (panel lines, screw heads, rivet rows, small chips); inner pod wall uses the cockpit lining so markings no longer show inside; guns and seat 3 weapons in a lighter FDE; engine 195 kW (was 150); nose steering lock fades with speed; rotor gauge moved back left.
- 0.15.21: pilot MFD mapped the stock way (was turned 90 deg); includes all 0.15.20 changes.
- 0.15.20: rotor gauge restyled as a copy of the stock throttle gauge (curved arc, carrot = set rpm, coloured bar + readout = actual rpm), moved right; helipad spawns back on; stronger rudder (airflow and prop wash); seat 3 heading taken from the barrel direction in the kit frame.
- 0.15.19: jumps removed. Custom axis = manual rotor rpm (110-450) on the ground; with flight assist on the rotor picks its own rpm (450 at full throttle on the takeoff roll, otherwise follows the throttle). HUD shows MANUAL / AUTO, green from 300 rpm.
- 0.15.18: no hop in place on the brakes (pre-spin held at 380 rpm, auto jump only when rolling free); body hangs level under the rotor and the stick has real roll authority; pitch stick commands g so it stays smooth at high speed.
- 0.15.17: rotor lifts like a full 8.4 m disc (less drag, holds level flight and climbs at 70-90 km/h); the jump holds the aircraft up while it builds speed and settles gently instead of dropping.
- 0.15.16: rotor thrust and the jump now act at the aircraft's centre of mass (no more nose-down with the stick back or backflip on the jump); thrust normal to the tilted rotor disc and scaled by rotor rpm; rotor pitch/roll damping; roll control from the rotor head; the wheels hold it upright on the takeoff roll.
- 0.15.15 test build: gyro rotor model. Each blade surface has its own lift transform pitched with the rotor head
  (3 deg neutral, 12 deg stick back, -1 deg pushed, held under the stall); blade lift scales with rotor rpm, which
  builds with airspeed (40 + 18 rpm per m/s, 380 max) or the pre-rotator. The artificial roll-out lift push is gone
  and the ground pitch torque is reduced. Flight log every second below 40 m (speed, climb, rotor rpm, lift share,
  incidence, body AoA, stick, throttle, prop thrust, attitude).
- 0.15.14 test build: SUB-2000 grip sunk into the frame (no gap at the joint), trigger guard ends buried in the frame and
  grip, trigger is a single smooth curve.
- 0.15.13 test build: SUB-2000 lower to the Kel-Tec shape - raked grip with straight back strap and flared base,
  molded square pockets cut into both sides, mag release button in a well, angled magazine base, thin strap trigger
  guard from the hinge pin to the grip front, slim curved trigger moved just ahead of the grip.
- 0.15.12 test build: SUB-2000 grip reshaped (rounded section, palm swell, finger grooves, sunk grip panels), round
  trigger guard and curved trigger; front block removed from the muzzle, shallow bore at the tip. Cockpit opening cut
  cleanly along the sill, old rim tubes removed, windscreen base strip seated on the wall edge all round, side consoles
  kept inside the skin. Copilot station: game controller style handset on a post (targeting pod and weapons) with his
  hands on it, small backup side stick, throttle and rudder pedals that follow the inputs.
- 0.15.11 test build: SUB-2000 slots, pockets and holes cut into the model (waffle pockets, M-LOK and rail slots,
  through-holes in the stock, bore, muzzle, rear sight aperture, sling cup) instead of raised colour patches.
- 0.15.10 test build: SUB-2000 detail - black rail with steel slots, dark waffle pockets, black grip inserts and butt
  pad, steel hinge pin, latch, trigger, sling cup, barrel bands, thread protector and clamp bolts, rear and front sights.
- 0.15.9 test build: rotor hub spins with the blades; blade lift area x1.6 and drag roughly halved; rotor assist on
  the takeoff roll held to 25 m/s; jump gauge moved to the right middle of the HUD as a vertical bar in the stock HUD
  font and colour; flight assist on + full throttle on the ground starts an automatic jump; spawns at revetments and
  shelters as well as medium hangars; takeoff-roll log (speed, climb, yaw rate).
- 0.15.8 test build: spawns at medium hangars only (helipads off for runway testing); takeoff stability: rotor lift
  centred just behind the centre of mass, larger fins, gentler pedal brake on the main wheels, main tyres slide before
  they trip the narrow track, gun mount mass moved to the centreline. SUB-2000 raised out of the seat sill with cleaner
  stock and frame; sensor housing blended into the belly with darker lens rings.
- 0.15.7 test build: folded SUB-2000 carbines on seats 1 and 2; HUD pre-rotator gauge (rpm, idle-to-full bar, red to
  green at jump speed); rotor head pitch authority on the takeoff roll; mast, engine and tail braced to the frame, stiffer
  main gear, less nose steering lock; faired sensor turret housing with side arm; square MFD with smaller six-pack; gauges
  moved to the cockpit camera layer so they draw.
- 0.15.6 test build: prop no longer stops below full throttle (strike-detector disc kept clear of the engine part,
  strike log added); custom axis 1 scaled to the binding's range; main gear casts clear of the nacelle so the wheels roll;
  gunner idle pose upright; darker rifle furniture; harnesses hide with their occupant / gunner kit; side seam removed;
  AGM-48 / IRM-S2 rail mounts equip again on lower pylons; six-pack flight instruments around a larger MFD; targeting
  ball rebuilt with flush lens windows.
- 0.15.5 test build: fixed the engine RPM loop (prop blades struck the tail boom collider) and the tail snapping at spawn
  (overlapping colliders now jointed); colliders skip thin hardware. Cockpit rework: square-shouldered cowl, wrap-round
  windscreen, portrait MFD, smaller gauges. Mid-belly sensor turret, beefed-up stub pylons with direct AGM-48/IRM-S2
  rails, FDE seat rifles, parked ejection seat hidden on bail-out, part-detach log.
- Blockout model scripts.
